# Shop policies

## Shipping

Shipping costs 5.00 plus 1.20 per started kilogram. Orders of 50.00 or more ship for free. The carrier refuses
parcels over 30 kg, so such orders cannot be placed.

## Refunds

Customers can ask for a refund up to 14 days after delivery, or at any time before the order ships. Refunds are
for the full amount. A support agent approves each request; the refund worker pays the approved ones every night.

## Discount codes

One code per order. `WELCOME10` takes 10% off the items, `SPRING5` takes 5.00 off. A discount never makes the
items cost less than zero, and shipping is computed on the price before the discount.
