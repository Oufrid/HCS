# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1. The summary
below is adapted from it; the full text at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/> applies.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Respect differing opinions and experience; disagree with ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community, not only for ourselves.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone officially
represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through GitHub (the repository owner, @Oufrid). Reports
are handled promptly and confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues
and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban a
contributor, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 (correction, warning, temporary
ban, permanent ban).
